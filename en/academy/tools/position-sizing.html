How to use this finance tool

This page turns a finance concept into checkable inputs, formulas, and scenarios. Read the variable definitions first, then compare conservative, base, and stress cases; one result is not a promise of return.

Suggested workflow: confirm units and time periods, enter your own assumptions, then review sensitivity, costs, and downside cases.

Limitations: the model does not forecast markets and may not include every tax, slippage, liquidity, credit, regulatory, or contract term. Verify important decisions with current primary information and a qualified professional.

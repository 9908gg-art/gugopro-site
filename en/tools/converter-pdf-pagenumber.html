Frequently asked questions

How do I write Page 1 of 8?

Enter Page {page} of {total}; the tokens are replaced per page.

Can I use custom text?

Yes. Enter any label and use the page and total tokens when needed.

Is the original PDF changed?

No. The tool creates a new output file.

Frequently asked questions

Are Taiwanese jin and Chinese jin the same?

This tool uses 0.6 kg for Taiwanese jin and 0.5 kg for Chinese jin as everyday reference factors, shown in each row.

Can I enter half a kilogram?

Yes. Simple fractions, mixed fractions and scientific notation are supported.

Are results sent to a remote service?

No. All mass conversions run locally in the current browser.

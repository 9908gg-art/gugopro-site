Frequently asked questions

Are contributions made at the start or end of a month?

This calculator models end-of-month contributions; different timing changes the result.

How should I read real value?

Nominal ending assets are divided by the compounded inflation factor to express purchasing power in today-like units.

Is this investment advice?

No. Return, inflation and time horizon are editable assumptions; actual market results can differ substantially.

Frequently asked questions

Must sound be enabled?

No. Sound is an optional cue; the timer still runs if the browser limits audio.

Does the timer run on a server?

No. Countdown, rounds and the Web Audio cue run in the local tab.

When does the long break appear?

After four focus rounds, the next phase automatically becomes a 15-minute long break.

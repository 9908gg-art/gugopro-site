Frequently asked questions

Why not use one multiplier?

Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin and Rankine have different zero points, so conversion needs an offset and a scale.

How do K and °C differ?

Kelvin starts at absolute zero and differs from Celsius by 273.15; their temperature intervals have the same scale.

Are negative temperatures supported?

Yes. Enter values such as -40 and all scales update together.

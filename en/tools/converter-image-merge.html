Frequently asked questions

How many images can I merge?

The practical limit depends on browser memory and Canvas dimensions; test with reasonable sizes first.

Does grid mode crop images?

No. Images are centered inside each cell with their ratio preserved; the selected background fills empty space.

Is the output lossless?

PNG uses lossless pixel encoding, although very large images may hit browser Canvas limits.

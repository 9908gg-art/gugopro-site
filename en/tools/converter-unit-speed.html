Frequently asked questions

How do I enter pace?

Enter 5:30 for five minutes thirty seconds per kilometre, or enter total seconds.

Is Mach fixed?

The tool uses approximately 343 m/s for dry air at 20°C as an everyday reference; actual sound speed varies with conditions.

Can speed use fractions?

Yes. Speed rows support simple fractions and scientific notation.

Features and quick guide

Why cut with a waveform?

This tool turns the full audio amplitude into a readable waveform and connects the selected start/end times to handles and preview controls. Decoding, trimming, and WAV/MP3 packaging stay in your browser.

3-step quick guide

  1. 1
    Choose or drop an audio file and wait for local decoding and waveform rendering.
  2. 2
    Drag the start and end handles, then preview the selection to confirm the range.
  3. 3
    Choose WAV or MP3, export the clip, and download the local result.

Zero-upload privacy promise

Audio is processed in this browser with Web Audio API, Canvas, and Blob; it is never sent to a server.

No upload endpoint is used; speed depends on your device and browser support.

Frequently asked questions

Which formats are supported?

Actual decoding depends on the browser. WAV, MP3, and OGG are common; M4A support varies by browser.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. File API reads the file in this tab, and the exported result is a local Blob.

Can I export MP3?

Yes. MP3 is encoded in the browser; WAV is the faster choice on lower-resource devices.

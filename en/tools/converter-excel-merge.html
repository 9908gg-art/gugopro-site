Privacy promise

PDF and spreadsheet bytes are parsed and exported only in this browser tab; they are never sent to a server.

No file-upload endpoint is used; speed depends on your device and browser support.

Frequently asked questions

How is CSV handled?

SheetJS reads CSV as one worksheet before combining it with the others.

Are duplicate sheet names overwritten?

No. The tool adds file names or sequence numbers to keep names unique.

Is the data uploaded?

No. Parsing and XLSX creation happen in this browser.

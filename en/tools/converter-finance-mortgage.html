Frequently asked questions

How is the grace period modeled?

Each grace-period month pays interest only and does not reduce principal.

Are prepayment penalties included?

No. The input is treated as an upfront principal reduction without bank-specific penalties.

Does this replace a bank quote?

No. Actual loans depend on rounding, fees, resets and underwriting.

Frequently asked questions

How are CODE128 and EAN-13 different?

CODE128 accepts general text and mixed content; EAN-13 is commonly used for 13-digit product identifiers.

Are digits sent to a server?

No. JsBarcode draws into an SVG element in the browser.

What happens with invalid input?

The page keeps the error state and asks you to check the digit length or format requirements.

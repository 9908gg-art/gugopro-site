Frequently asked questions

Can I extract pages instead of deleting them?

Yes. Switch the action to Extract selected pages.

What range syntax is accepted?

Single pages and inclusive ranges separated by commas are accepted.

Are encrypted PDFs supported?

Password-protected PDFs may not be readable by pdf-lib in the browser.

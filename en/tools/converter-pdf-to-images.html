Privacy promise

PDF, spreadsheet and document bytes are parsed and exported only in this browser tab; they are never sent to a server.

No file-upload endpoint is used; speed depends on your device and browser support.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF uploaded?

No. pdf.js reads the PDF bytes only in this browser tab.

Can I download one page?

Yes. After rendering, download the first page or use the ZIP for all pages.

What limits high resolution?

Larger pages use more memory; the practical limit depends on the device and browser.

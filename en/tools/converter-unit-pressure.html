Frequently asked questions

Can hPa and bar be converted?

Yes. 1 bar = 100,000 Pa and 1 hPa = 100 Pa; both appear in the matrix.

Are mmHg and Torr identical?

They are often treated as close in everyday data, but the tool lists distinct reference values to avoid confusion.

Is it suitable for tyre psi?

It is useful for quick conversion; follow the vehicle or equipment manufacturer for actual inflation settings.

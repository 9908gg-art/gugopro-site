Frequently asked questions

How are shares estimated?

Required principal is divided by the entered share price and rounded up; lot sizes and market rules are not modeled.

Is yield fixed?

The calculator uses a fixed yield as a scenario assumption; actual distributions can change or stop.

Is passive income guaranteed?

No. This is a planning and sensitivity tool, not a promise of future cash flow.

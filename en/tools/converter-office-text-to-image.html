Frequently asked questions

Does it use source files?

No. It only lays out the text you enter on a local Canvas.

Can I copy the image?

Browsers with image clipboard support can copy it directly; otherwise download the PNG.

Can I change the image dimensions?

The canvas uses a share-friendly width and extends its height locally based on line count.

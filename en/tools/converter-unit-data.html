Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between KB and KiB?

KB uses powers of 1000, while KiB uses powers of 1024; the matrix keeps them separate.

How are bits and bytes related?

1 byte = 8 bits; the matrix shows the exact conversion.

Can it handle very large capacities?

Scientific notation and BigInt-backed rational arithmetic support large storage values.

Frequently asked questions

Does fading change pitch?

No. It changes sample amplitude without changing the sample rate or playback speed.

Can fade seconds exceed the clip length?

The tool caps each effect within the clip duration to avoid unexpected overlap.

Does MP3 export require an upload?

No. The MP3 encoder runs locally in the browser and exposes a downloadable Blob.

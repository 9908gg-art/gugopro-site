Frequently asked questions

Are US and imperial gallons the same?

No. The tool keeps separate standard factors for US and imperial gallons.

What does oil barrel mean here?

An oil barrel uses 42 US gallons, approximately 158.987294928 litres.

Can I use scientific notation?

Yes. Values such as 2.5e-4 L are supported.

Frequently asked questions

Must funnel values decrease?

No, but a typical conversion funnel decreases from top to bottom; rebounds are still drawn as entered.

Are conversion rates calculated?

Widths scale against the maximum stage; original values remain visible in each step label.

Can this make financial decisions?

It is a visualization aid, not a business or investment guarantee; interpret it with complete operating data.

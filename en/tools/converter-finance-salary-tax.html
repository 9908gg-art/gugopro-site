Frequently asked questions

Is this a filing result?

No. It models a single filer with standard deduction and salary income only; spouse, special deductions, credits, insurance and non-salary income are excluded.

How do dependents affect the estimate?

Each dependent adds one illustrative exemption; age and eligibility variations are not expanded in this simplified model.

Which tax year is used?

The page uses Taiwan 2026 / 115年度 official amounts, applicable to filing 115年度 income in May 2027; verify current rules before filing.

Frequently asked questions

Can it extract scanned PDFs?

Scans without a text layer need OCR, which this tool does not run.

Is reading order exact?

Single-column documents work best; verify multi-column or positioned content.

Is the text uploaded?

No. Extraction stays in the current browser tab.

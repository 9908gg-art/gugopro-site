Frequently asked questions

How is the minimum fee applied?

Each side uses the larger of percentage commission and the minimum commission.

Does breakeven include selling costs?

Yes. Selling commission and transaction tax are included in the exit price calculation.

Does this work for every market?

Not automatically. Replace rates, discount, minimums and tax with the applicable broker and market rules.

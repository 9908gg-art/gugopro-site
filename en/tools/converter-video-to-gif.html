Frequently asked questions

Why use a short clip?

Every GIF frame is encoded in the browser, so shorter clips and smaller widths reduce memory pressure.

Does the GIF include audio?

No. The GIF format does not contain an audio track.

Can I use 30 FPS?

Yes. The control allows 10–30 FPS; higher FPS takes longer and creates a larger file.

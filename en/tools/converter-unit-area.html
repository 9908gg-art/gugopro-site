Frequently asked questions

What are the ping, jia and fen references?

The tool uses 1 ping = 400/121 m², 1 jia = 2,934 ping and 1 fen = 1/10 jia as common Taiwan land references.

Is it suitable for cadastral contracts?

It is useful for estimates and data preparation; official transactions and surveys should follow the governing standard.

Does it suffer floating-point drift?

Core conversions use BigInt-backed rational arithmetic and controlled formatting to avoid ordinary binary-float accumulation.

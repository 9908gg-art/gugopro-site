Frequently asked questions

What content is supported?

You can encode text and URLs directly, or build Wi-Fi and vCard payloads from simple fields.

Is the QR Code uploaded?

No. The QRCode library creates the Canvas/SVG result in the current page.

Which error correction should I choose?

M works for short content; Q or H can help when a printed code may be partly covered.

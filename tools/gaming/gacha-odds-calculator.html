Tutorial: Soft Pity & 50/50 Mechanics

What is Soft Pity?

Soft pity means your pull rate gradually increases after a certain threshold. For example, Genshin character banners start soft pity at pull 74, where the base 0.6% rate increases by 6% per pull until the hard pity at 90.

  • Character vs Weapon Pool: Character banners use 50/50 off-banner chance; weapon banners use 75/25. This tool precisely models both.
  • Hard Pity: At the cap, you are guaranteed to get the target item.
  • Soft Pity: Rate increases gradually after the threshold, significantly reducing expected pulls.
  • 50/50: After hitting, 50% chance it's the UP item, 50% off-banner. If off-banner, next hit is guaranteed UP.
  • 75/25: Weapon banner: 25% chance UP, 75% off-banner. If off-banner, next hit is guaranteed.

How to Check Your Pity Count

Your "pity count" (水位) is the number of pulls since your last 5-star pull on the current banner. Here's how to find it:

  • Genshin Impact: Open Wish History from the banner page. Count how many pulls have passed since your last 5-star.
  • Honkai: Star Rail: Open Warp History from the Warp page. Count pulls since your last 5-star.
  • Wuthering Waves: Check Echo History / Convoy History. Count pulls since last 5-star echo.
  • Arknights: Check recruitment history in the Recruitment terminal.
  • Blue Archive / Uma Musume: These use a spark system (200 pulls = exchange), so pity count is less critical. Just check total pulls on current banner.
